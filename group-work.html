REFLECTIVE BLOG / 02

Growing through collaboration.

Group work is more than the division of tasks. It is an opportunity to understand different perspectives, navigate challenges and discover what it means to work towards a shared objective.

GROUP WORK EXPERIENCE • TUCKMAN'S MODEL
01

Understanding the dynamics of a team

Bruce Tuckman's model explains how groups develop through five stages: Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing and Adjourning. Each stage represents a different part of the process of becoming an effective team.

01

STAGE ONE

Forming

The beginning of the group's journey. Members meet, become familiar with one another, discuss the task and establish initial expectations.

Personal reflection to be added

Describe how the group was formed, your first impressions, how responsibilities were introduced and how the group approached the assignment.

02

STAGE TWO

Storming

Differences in opinions, working styles and expectations may emerge. The group learns to navigate disagreements and challenges.

Personal reflection to be added

Explain whether the group experienced disagreements, communication difficulties or unequal participation, and how these situations were addressed.

03

STAGE THREE

Norming

Members begin to establish trust, understand their responsibilities and develop a shared approach to completing the task.

Personal reflection to be added

Describe how the group improved its communication, distributed responsibilities and developed a better working relationship.

04

STAGE FOUR

Performing

The group becomes more focused and productive. Members work together effectively towards achieving their shared objective.

Personal reflection to be added

Explain how the group completed its work, what your contribution was and what helped the team achieve its objectives.

05

STAGE FIVE

Adjourning

The group concludes its work, evaluates the experience and reflects on the outcome and lessons gained from working together.

Personal reflection to be added

Reflect on the final outcome, what you learned about teamwork and what you would approach differently in future group tasks.

LOOKING BACK

Collaboration is a skill built through experience.

The effectiveness of a team is not determined only by the abilities of its individual members, but also by how they communicate, adapt and contribute towards a common purpose.
