PROFESSIONAL EMAIL ETIQUETTE

Before pressing send.

  • Use a clear and relevant subject line.
  • Address the recipient professionally.
  • Keep the message concise and purposeful.
  • Attach the correct documents.
  • Check spelling, grammar and recipient details.
  • Use a professional email address and signature.
